Alathar — support

Alathar (الأثر) brings a daily authentic hadith, athkar at their times, the Qur'an with tajweed and Tafsir as-Sa'di, Qur'anic stories and prayer times — in Arabic, English and Urdu, with no account and no ads.

Prayer times look wrong?

On the prayer page, pick your city or "Use my location"; you can also change the calculation method and Asr school.

Not getting reminders?

Allow notifications for Alathar in your phone's settings and turn reminders on in the app's Settings.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone only. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Found a mistake in a text, translation or explanation?

Please email us with the hadith or verse and your note.
